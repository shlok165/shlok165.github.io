Interval Quiz

Interval is an online quiz and exam platform built as a Human-Computer Interaction course project at IIT Ropar. Instructors create courses, question banks and timed quizzes; students take them in the browser and see results when the instructor releases them.

Access is limited to members of IIT Ropar. Students and staff can sign in with their @iitrpr.ac.in Google account or with an institute email and password.
