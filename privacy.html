Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy covers the Interval quiz platform, an IIT Ropar course project used for quizzes and exams by IIT Ropar students and staff.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, institute email address and, if provided, your entry number. If you use email and password sign-in, we store only a salted hash of your password.
  • Google sign-in: if you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address and whether Google has verified that email. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Quiz activity: your answers, answer history, submissions, scores and any appeals.
  • Exam integrity events: during a quiz, the site records events such as leaving the tab, exiting full screen and copy or paste attempts, along with the IP address an attempt was started from. Instructors can review these for their own courses.

How it is used

Only to run quizzes: signing you in, saving and grading your answers, showing results, and letting your instructors and teaching assistants manage their courses. We do not sell data, show advertising or share data with third parties.

Who can see it

You, plus the instructors and teaching assistants of courses you are enrolled in, and the platform administrators.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in the platform's own database and backups, and is kept for the duration of the course and its assessment period. You can ask for your account and data to be deleted using the contact below.
